clarity.

How this website works and what happens to your information.

Information you send us

When you request a table, send a party or corporate enquiry, reserve a spot at an event, place an order, request a gift voucher, or leave feedback, the details you enter (such as your name, phone number, email address, date, and message) are sent to Nasha Resto Bar and stored so the team can respond and manage your visit. We use them only for that purpose. Feedback is published only after review, showing the name you gave.

Guest photos

If you share a photo on the gallery page, the team sees it with the name and caption you gave before deciding whether to publish it. Only approved photos appear on the website, shown with your name and caption. Before sending, your browser makes a smaller copy of the photo, which removes the location and camera details stored inside it. To have a photo taken down, contact the restaurant.

Offers and updates

We send offers and event news only if you sign up or tick the box asking for them. To stop, reply to any message or contact the restaurant and we will remove you.

Confirmations

We may contact you by WhatsApp, SMS, email, or phone about a booking, enquiry, or order you made. These messages may be delivered through a messaging provider acting on our behalf.

Saved on your device

Saved menu items, your order basket, and your answer to the age question are stored in your browser’s local storage on your device. Clearing this site’s browser data removes them. This website does not use advertising cookies.

Visitor counts

We count page views and an approximate number of daily visitors to understand how the website is used. The count uses a short, one-way code derived from your connection details that changes every day and is not used to identify you or follow you across other websites. No third-party analytics or advertising trackers are used.

External services

Typefaces and photographs are served directly from this website. The map loads from Google, and Instagram posts load from Instagram, only after you choose to load them. Online payments, when offered, are completed with the payment provider; we do not see or store your card details. Booking-partner, delivery-partner, and directions links open third-party services with their own privacy practices.

Age

This website promotes a venue that serves alcohol and asks visitors to confirm they are of legal drinking age. Alcohol is served only to guests of legal drinking age, and is not sold through online ordering.

Restaurant details

Opening hours, menu, prices, offers, and events are maintained by the restaurant and can change. A table, event place, or party booking is confirmed only when the restaurant confirms it with you.
